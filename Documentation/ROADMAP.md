# Prototype v0.3 priorities

1. **Unaided Windows playtests and accessibility.** Observe new players finding supplies, leaving the furnished house and following the ridge trail. Test real mouse/keyboard, alternate layouts, ultrawide and low resolutions. Add remappable keys, FOV, optional camera shake/head bob and caption/HUD scaling. Preserve opening/countdown/flood/restart regression coverage.
2. **Representative performance pass.** Profile a standalone player on lower and mid-range PCs, capture CPU/GPU/GC and draw calls, then add quality presets. Reduce unnecessary real-time lights/material variants, audit unused template inference packages, batch static props and add vegetation LODs/culling. Do not remove packages required by existing Editor tools without checking dependencies.
3. **Presentation and sound.** Replace robotic placeholder speech with verified freely licensed or original human recordings, separate speech/effects/ambience volume controls, improve indoor/outdoor mixing, evacuation body language, dam-fracture art and foam/splash contact. Keep the original television program or use only properly licensed original replacements.
4. **More convincing bounded flood geography.** Keep deterministic local water queries and a predictable safe height. Add authored valley channels/front variation and building masking so water does not appear through every room. Add small safe pockets and more readable current cues; avoid full CFD or expensive destruction physics.
5. **Small first-night shelter slice after evacuation remains stable.** Give food, water and the radio meaningful shelter interactions, add one checkpoint and short environmental stories. Validate pacing before planning the complete five-day simulation. Vehicle driving, boats, combat, enemies, multiplayer and complex crafting remain out of this milestone.

The v0.2 upgrade already adds the couch/cartoon/news opening, speech/subtitles, furniture, parked vehicles, animated residents, expanded inventory, batteries/radio, moving flood front, swimming, current and floating debris. Detailed constraints and verification are in `V02_COMPLETION.md`; prior v0.1 roadmap is archived in `Archive/v0.1Source/ROADMAP.md`.
