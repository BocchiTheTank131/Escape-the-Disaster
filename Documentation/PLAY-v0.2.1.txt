FLOOD SURVIVAL: 5 DAYS — PROTOTYPE 0.2.1
Wooded valley / roof-level flood / improved television

Run FloodSurvival.exe. Extract the whole ZIP and keep the runtime folders/DLLs together.

START STORY: look around from the couch while the original cartoon plays.
News interrupts after 12 seconds and starts the full 150-second warning.
Press E to stand immediately. Gather optional supplies, then follow the amber earth trail east.
The shelter building is gone. The trail leads to open wooded high ground, about 15 m high.
After the breach, the flood front travels downstream and rises for 75 seconds to 10.6 m.
Even the tallest house roof is reached. Stay on dry ground above 11.6 m to survive.
Other sufficiently high dry hills also count; no shelter proximity is required.

WASD move / mouse look / Shift sprint / Space jump or hold to swim up.
E stand or take targeted supply / I inspect / Tab bag / Escape pause or close bag.
F flashlight / R batteries / H first aid / V emergency radio.
Use food, water and first aid from the bag. Base capacity 6; backpack expands it to 12.
Inventory browsing does not pause the timer. Pause freezes gameplay and audio.
Replay skip unlocks after seeing the introduction.

The TV now has an animated original cartoon and a fictional live news presenter,
dam diagram, headlines and scrolling evacuation ticker. No outside TV footage is used.

Free Kenney and Poly Haven content; see ASSET_CREDITS.md and Licenses/.
Terrain, pine meshes and television graphics are authored for this project.
This is the first evacuation prototype, not the complete five-day simulation.
